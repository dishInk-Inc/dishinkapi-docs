## What is Smart Invoice?

ZRA Smart Invoice is Zambia Revenue Authority's mandatory electronic fiscal system for VAT-registered businesses. Every sale must be submitted to ZRA in real time and returned with a unique receipt number, digital signature, and QR code before a valid tax invoice can be issued to a customer.

DishInk integrates directly with ZRA Smart Invoice — cashiers never need to think about it. When a payment is completed at the POS, DishInk automatically submits the sale to ZRA, receives the fiscal signature, and prints the verified receipt. The entire process takes under two seconds.

## Two tax modes

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="ZRA Smart Invoice (VAT)">
    For VAT-registered businesses. Every sale is fiscalised in real time through ZRA's Smart Invoice system.

    **What you get:**

    - Receipt number from ZRA on every sale
    - Verified digital signature
    - Scannable QR code for inspector verification
    - VAT breakdown per tax category (Standard 16%, Exempt, Zero-rated)
    - Full audit log for regulatory review

    **Requirements:**

    - Active TPIN from ZRA
    - ZRA Smart Invoice device approval
    - Device initialised in DishInk (one-time setup)

    **Receipt header shows:** `TAX INVOICE`
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Turnover Tax (SME)">
    For smaller businesses that are not VAT-registered. No ZRA device required.

    **What you get:**

    - Sales receipt with turnover tax calculated automatically
    - ZRA-compliant receipt format
    - Full transaction history

    **Requirements:**

    - None — works immediately after setting your tax rate

    **Receipt header shows:** `SALES RECEIPT`
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Switch modes under **Settings → Billing and Tax → Tax Mode**.

## The setup process at a glance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your ZRA approval">
    Register on the ZRA Smart Invoice Taxpayer Portal and obtain your device serial number and TPIN. ZRA approves your registration before you can proceed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your device in DishInk">
    Go to **ZRA Smart Invoice → Devices → Add Device**. Enter your TPIN, branch code, and device serial number. DishInk calls ZRA to complete the one-time handshake.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map your menu items">
    Assign UNSPSC classification codes and VAT categories to each menu item under **ZRA Smart Invoice → Item Mapping**. This tells ZRA what type of goods you are selling.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sync items to ZRA">
    Click **Sync to ZRA** to register your mapped items. ZRA must receive item registrations before they can appear on fiscal receipts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start taking payments">
    From this point everything is automatic. Every sale at the POS is fiscalised without any action from the cashier.
  </Step>
</Steps>
